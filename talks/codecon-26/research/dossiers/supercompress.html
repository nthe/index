Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

SuperCompress

  • Type: Query-aware compressor.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. The product compresses context before inference.
  • Baseline and test: B5 coding-agent suite, 24 cases versus original context.
  • Savings scope: The 64.1% figure is the mean reduction of the submitted context in 24 benchmark cases. It does not measure all tokens in complete agent sessions. The query is excluded from compression. Session-wide savings are not reported.
  • Token volume: 64.1% mean context cut.
  • Cost: Not found in cited source.
  • Latency: 5.8 s p50 compression, not end-to-end.
  • Accuracy or quality: 24/24 evidence containment; no downstream accuracy.

Across all 390 cases the mean cut is about 3.6%.

Source: Product documentation and benchmark.
