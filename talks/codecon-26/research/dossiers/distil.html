Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

Distil

  • Type: Agent context compression.
  • Reduction purpose: Main; Distil compresses context and measures its effect on cost and decisions.
  • Baseline and test: Project test on decision-determined agent corpus; LLM-graded.
  • Token volume: 83.2% savings in compressed blocks versus original blocks, across a synthetic 120-turn corpus. This is not a complete agent-session token comparison.
  • Cost: Not established as cost saving.
  • Latency: Claimed roughly 1,000× faster than nearest tool; not baseline end-to-end.
  • Accuracy or quality: 0% observed decision changes; authors report confidence bound.

Decision equivalence is a proxy for task quality; check corpus and confidence methodology.

Sources: Benchmark; benchmark methodology; product.
