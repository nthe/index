base76 token-compressor

  • Type: Prompt compression library.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. The library compresses prompts before they reach an LLM.
  • Baseline and test: The headline claim compares original and compressed prompts. Its workload is not specified. A separate benchmark compares five individual prompts, including four skipped prompts.
  • Token volume: Claims 30–70% fewer prompt tokens.
  • Cost: Not quantified.
  • Latency: Not quantified.
  • Accuracy or quality: Not quantified.

A compression range does not establish quality or total cost.

The headline claim applies to prompts, not a complete agent session. The separate five-prompt benchmark reports 39% fewer prompt tokens in total. It does not measure a complete session or include all compression overhead.
