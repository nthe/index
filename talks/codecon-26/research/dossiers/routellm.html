Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

RouteLLM

  • Type: Model routing framework.
  • Reduction purpose: Secondary / indirect. The main purpose is to reduce LLM cost by routing requests to cheaper models. It does not compress prompts.
  • Savings scope: The authors report the headline cost claim across benchmark queries, including MT Bench. Each query is routed between models. A complete agent session or workflow is not evaluated. The published paper uses a separate cost analysis and reports lower savings for its tested router settings.
  • Baseline and test: Always use strong model versus routing between strong and weak models.
  • Token volume: Not reported.
  • Cost: Up to 85% lower while retaining 95% of strong-model performance.
  • Latency: Not reported.
  • Accuracy or quality: 95% of GPT-4 performance under authors’ benchmark definition.

Routing can lower price without reducing token volume; performance retention is not percentage-point accuracy.

Sources: Product; authors’ benchmark claim; paper and separate cost analysis.
