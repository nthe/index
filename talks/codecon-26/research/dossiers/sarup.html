Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

sarup

  • Type: Thai-first MCP context compression.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. The server compresses context and stores originals for retrieval.
  • Savings scope: Individual content samples. The −50.8% and −88.1% figures compare compression modes on one 10-sentence Thai paragraph. They do not measure a complete agent session. The automatic hook handles large Read, Bash, and Grep outputs; small outputs and source code pass through.
  • Token volume: −50.8% to −88.1% on the sample paragraph, depending on mode; counted with cl100k_base.
  • Cost: n/a.
  • Latency: Compression time is reported by mode; an end-to-end comparison is n/a.
  • Accuracy or quality: Original text is recoverable from storage. This does not establish task accuracy for the compressed view.

Source: Author repository and measured results.
