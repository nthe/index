TokenLean

  • Type: Optimization proxy.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. Token and cost reduction are the proxy’s primary goals.
  • Savings scope: The headline and rerun aggregate input-token savings across quality-gated datasets, including agentic payloads. They exclude output tokens. The source does not establish full-session coverage. The cost figure is an estimate for the earlier gated workload, not an invoice comparison.
  • Baseline and test: Internal quality-gated ablation versus direct requests.
  • Token volume: 54.1% earlier; 26.4% in later rerun.
  • Cost: About 70% estimated in earlier gated set.
  • Latency: Not found in cited source.
  • Accuracy or quality: Quality-gated subset; no general accuracy figure.

The original harness is not shipped; excluding failed datasets biases aggregate. Workloads and dates differ.
