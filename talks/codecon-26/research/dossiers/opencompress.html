Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

OpenCompress

  • Type: Compression proxy.
  • Purpose: Main. The product compresses LLM calls to reduce token use and cost.
  • Baseline and test: The vendor claims about 50% token savings for Claude Code sessions and 60–70% cost savings for enterprise chatbots. It does not give a session benchmark, baseline, or billing method for these claims.
  • Token volume: About 50% claimed for agent sessions; measurement scope and method are unclear.
  • Cost: 60–70% claimed for enterprise chatbots; measurement scope and method are unclear.
  • Latency: Not found in cited source.
  • Accuracy or quality: Not found in cited source.

The vendor also reports a playground test that compares individual original and compressed calls. The agent-trace example reports 46% fewer input tokens, 83% fewer output tokens, and 72% lower cost for one call. It does not measure a complete agent workflow. The documentation does not state whether the cost figure includes the service fee.

Sources: Product claims; pipeline and playground results; pricing.
