Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

TrueFoundry auto routing

  • Type: Model routing.
  • Baseline and test: Vendor compares 550 individual prompts from 11 graded datasets against routing every request to Opus 5. A larger test includes about 4,700 calls across 16 datasets.
  • Token volume: Not found in cited source.
  • Cost: $3.23 → $0.99 (−69%).
  • Latency: Mean 7.6 → 4.0 seconds.
  • Accuracy or quality: Pass rate 96.4% → 94.2% (−2.2 pp); 98% relative quality retained.
  • Reduction purpose: Secondary / indirect. Auto Routing reduces cost by selecting models; it is a feature of the AI gateway and does not compress prompts.
  • Savings scope: Cost, latency, and quality figures cover the 550 individual benchmark prompts. They do not measure complete agent sessions. A separate test reports cost on production-shaped agent and chat prompts without quality grading.
