Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

Portkey AI Gateway

  • Type: Exact and semantic response cache within an AI gateway.
  • Reduction purpose: Secondary. The gateway also provides routing, reliability, and observability. Caching avoids repeated model calls; it does not compress prompts.
  • Baseline and test: The vendor claims up to 20× cheaper and faster cache-served requests. It does not specify a benchmark, baseline, or workload for this headline.
  • Savings scope: Cache-served requests, not all requests or a complete agent session. Total savings depend on cache hits and misses. A separate vendor report claims an average 38% LLM cost reduction from caching across gateway traffic, but does not give the comparison method.
  • Token volume: Not quantified.
  • Cost: Up to −95% implied by the 20× claim; baseline unclear.
  • Latency: Up to 20× faster claimed; baseline unclear.
  • Accuracy or quality: Not quantified as a comparative delta.

Sources: Portkey cache documentation; Portkey semantic cache article; Portkey gateway production report.
