Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

Paritok

  • Reduction purpose: Main. Paritok-4B compresses coding-agent context.
  • Savings scope: Compressed context per instance in a single-shot SWE-bench Lite test. The test has one model call, no agent loop, and no recall. Complete multi-turn session savings are n/a.
  • Token volume: −74.3% context.
  • Cost: n/a.
  • Latency: n/a.
  • Accuracy or quality: n/a.

Authors report 300 SWE-bench Lite single-shot instances with compressed context at 25.7% of original size and 86.5% relative solve quality retained. Relative retention is not an absolute percentage-point delta. No matched net cost or latency is established in that result.
