Garnet-Owl token-compression

  • Reduction purpose: Main. This research code tests prompt compression to reduce token use.
  • Savings scope: One prompt per log question. The −25.3% compares compressed and original prompts for 150 questions on synthetic 30-line logs. Complete agent-session savings are not reported.
  • Token volume: −25.3% prompt.
  • Cost: n/a.
  • Latency: n/a.
  • Accuracy or quality: −4 pp accuracy.

Research implementation: 150 held-out log questions, fine-tuned Qwen3-1.7B full prompt 1,459 tokens and 77% versus compressed 1,090 and 73%.
