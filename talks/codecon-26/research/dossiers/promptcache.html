Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

PromptCache

  • Type: Prompt caching service.
  • Reduction purpose: Secondary or indirect. The main purpose is to reduce cost and latency with semantic caching, not to compress prompts. Cache hits avoid generation calls.
  • Savings scope: The headline cost claim does not state a benchmark workload, baseline, or cache hit rate. Full-session savings are not reported. The cache micro-benchmarks measure individual operations, not full requests.
  • Baseline and test: Repository headline; comparative workload not specified.
  • Token volume: Not quantified.
  • Cost: Claims up to 80% lower cost; workload and method are not stated.
  • Latency: Sub-millisecond cache operations are reported; no end-to-end comparison is specified.
  • Accuracy or quality: Not quantified.

Cache response time is not end-to-end latency.

Sources: Product or authors; method or additional source.
