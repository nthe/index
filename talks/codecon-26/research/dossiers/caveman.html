Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

Caveman

  • Type: Agent response compression.
  • Purpose: Main. Caveman reduces agent token use through shorter responses and other compression tools.
  • Baseline and test: Project headline versus normal responses.
  • Token volume: About 65% output-token reduction claimed in the repository headline. The project says no reviewed raw result is published for this comparison. Its separate ten-prompt test reports a 3% median output reduction against an instruction to answer concisely.
  • Savings scope: Output responses, not complete agent sessions. The ten-prompt test measures output length only; it excludes instruction overhead and input tokens. Whole-session savings for the headline claim are not reported.
  • Cost: Not found in cited source.
  • Latency: Not found in cited source.
  • Accuracy or quality: Not found in cited source.

Instruction overhead can make net savings negative. The project also reports separate tests of other components and full sessions; these do not establish the headline output claim.

Sources: Product repository; project measurement notes.
