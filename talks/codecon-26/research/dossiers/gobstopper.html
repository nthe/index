Gobstopper

  • Purpose: Main. The proxy compacts agent context to save tokens and preserves the full history locally.
  • Savings scope: Aggregate provider-reported input across 89 complete benchmark agent task runs; the token delta excludes output. Cost is the provider-reported total across those runs. Accuracy is the share of tasks solved. These are not individual tool-output comparisons.
  • Token volume: −29% input, mostly cache reads.
  • Cost: −16%.
  • Latency: n/a.
  • Accuracy or quality: +1.1 pp solved.

Retired project. One trial per arm on 89 Terminal-Bench 2.1 tasks: 60 → 61 solved, 118.6M → 84.3M input tokens, and $6.82 → $5.72. The cost interval includes an increase; 21 trials may have used an earlier build. Threshold differs from the default.
