Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

LongLLMLingua

  • Type: Query-aware prompt compression.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. Prompt compression is the method’s central purpose.
  • Savings scope: Individual benchmark prompts and responses, not complete agent sessions. The NaturalQuestions figure compares prompt tokens. The separate LooGLE cost estimate covers GPT-3.5-Turbo input and output for 1,000 samples; it does not include a compressor cost. Latency covers compression plus the LLM request for the tested prompts.
  • Baseline and test: Full prompt baseline; NaturalQuestions and separate LooGLE and latency experiments.
  • Token volume: About 4× compression on NaturalQuestions.
  • Cost: Up to 94% lower estimated GPT-3.5-Turbo inference cost in a separate LooGLE case.
  • Latency: 1.4–2.6× end-to-end speedup at 2–6× compression for roughly 10k-token prompts.
  • Accuracy or quality: Up to 21.4 percentage points improvement on NaturalQuestions with GPT-3.5-Turbo.

Figures are from different experiments and must not be treated as one result.

Sources: Product or authors; benchmark methods and cost estimates.
