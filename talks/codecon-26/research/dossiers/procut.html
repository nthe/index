Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

ProCut

  • Purpose: Main. ProCut compresses prompt templates by removing low-impact segments.
  • Savings scope: Individual production prompt templates in two use cases. The reported reductions do not measure total agent-session or workflow tokens. The paper also estimates inference savings per million calls; it does not report a total workflow cost delta.
  • Token volume: −78% production prompt.
  • Cost: n/a.
  • Latency: n/a.
  • Accuracy or quality: n/a.

The abstract reports 78% fewer prompt tokens. The paper reports −73% for intent classification and −84% for qualification assessment, compared with the original prompts. It reports preserved accuracy for the first and slightly improved performance for the second, without absolute deltas. The qualification test used 46 labeled examples. Compression latency is compared to another estimator, not the uncompressed pipeline.

Source: Author paper, section 5 and table 5.
