Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

Tokelang

  • Type: Prompt compiler.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. The product compiles prompts to reduce model input tokens.
  • Savings scope: The −34.29% claim covers the 8 compiled prompts only, not a complete agent session. The two passthrough prompts are excluded. Across all 10 prompts in the published table, the reduction is 28.27% (108 of 382 tokens); this excludes the decode system prompt and other workflow tokens.
  • Baseline and test: Curated 10-prompt set; 8 compiled and 2 passed through.
  • Token volume: 34.29% weighted savings among 8 compiled cases.
  • Cost: Not found in cited source.
  • Latency: 9.27 ms mean HTTP compilation latency; not full request.
  • Accuracy or quality: Not found in cited source.

Do not generalize compiled-only saving to all inputs.
