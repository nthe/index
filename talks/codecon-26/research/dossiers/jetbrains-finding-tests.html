Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

JetBrains finding-tests

  • Type: Rider skill and MCP tool for C# test discovery.
  • Reduction purpose: Secondary. The main purpose is to find the relevant test; token and cost savings are claimed benefits.
  • Baseline and scope: One project-reported C# test-writing task, with and without coverage-guided lookup. It is one complete task trajectory, not a broad session estimate.
  • Token volume: −39.5% usage-counted tokens (1.01M → 612K) in that trajectory. JetBrains reports up to −50% in its internal tests.
  • Cost: n/a; the article does not give a numeric cost delta for that trajectory.
  • Latency: n/a; coverage collection adds about 30 seconds on a small or medium solution and can take much longer.
  • Accuracy or quality: n/a; both example runs produced an equivalent passing test, but there is no quantitative task-quality comparison.

In a July 2026 update, JetBrains said it could not reproduce the savings with newer GPT-5.5-medium and Sonnet 4.6-high configurations. The skill became opt-in while JetBrains retunes it. The repository star count and activity refer to the shared Rider Skills repository; the observed August commit changed another skill.

Sources: JetBrains test and update; skill.

No independent quantitative benchmark was found. A user issue reports that the tool ran the full test suite; it does not measure savings.
