Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

tokenfold

  • Type: Structured context compression and optional relevance selection.
  • Purpose: Main. The product fits context into a token budget using structural compression and selection.
  • Savings scope: Individual fixtures and prompts, including tool schemas and a 30-record API response. Complete agent-session savings are not reported. The opt-in pruning showcase stores omitted rows for later retrieval; its savings exclude later retrieval.
  • Baseline and test: Original structured data or compact JSON versus compressed data; exact o200k_base counts and JSON-value recovery.
  • Token volume: −45.6% to −63.9% across two structural fixtures. Separate answer diagnostic: −32.2% provider prompt tokens for folded JSON; −40.1% for selection.
  • Cost: Not quantified.
  • Latency: Not quantified for an end-to-end workflow.
  • Accuracy or quality: Folded JSON: −25 pp (6/8 versus 8/8 correct); selection: 0 pp (16/16 versus 16/16), in small synthetic diagnostics. The two trials per task are not independent tasks.

Lossless decoding does not prove that a model interprets the encoded content correctly. The fixtures and diagnostics do not measure full-session results.

Sources: Product and benchmarks; Downstream-answer diagnostic.
