Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

Selective Context

  • Type: Prompt compression library.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. The method removes redundant input context.
  • Savings scope: The 50% figure applies to compressed input context in benchmark tasks. The inference-time figure comes from a single summarization example and excludes context construction. The quality figures cover downstream task results. Complete agent-session savings: n/a.
  • Baseline and test: Paper compares half-length context with full context across four applications.
  • Token volume: 50% less context.
  • Cost: Not reported.
  • Latency: 32% less inference time; compression overhead not established here.
  • Accuracy or quality: BERTScore −0.023 and faithfulness −0.038.

The paper reports 110.8 ms/token for full context and 76.3 ms/token for compressed context in the example. Context construction took 46.1 ms. Inference time is not end-to-end latency.
