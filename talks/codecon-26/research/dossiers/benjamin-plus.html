Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

Benjamin-Plus

  • Type: Coding-agent instruction skill.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. The rules aim to reduce token use and cost.
  • Baseline and scope: JetBrains' v6 test compares injected rules with no skill across 80 paired SkillsBench tasks. These are full task sessions. Results are project-reported.
  • Token volume: −21.9% median total tokens per task.
  • Cost: −17.9% median per task; −16.7% across totals.
  • Latency: −15.6% median task time.
  • Accuracy or quality: +0.03 mean verifier score (0.362 → 0.392); 7 tasks improved, 5 worsened and 68 tied. The test does not prove equal quality.

The prior-day comparison found −10.0% median cost, not statistically significant. A Java/Codex evaluation is described in the project report, but its evaluator is not identified. An external review repeats the results; it does not report a separate test.

Sources: Project; benchmark; external review.
