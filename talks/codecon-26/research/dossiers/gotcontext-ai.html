Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

gotcontext.ai

  • Type: MCP compression service.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. The service compresses context sent to LLMs.
  • Baseline and test: Vendor production compression calls and a large-document benchmark. They compare compressed content with its original input, not complete agent sessions.
  • Token volume: About 50% typical; peak 87.4%.
  • Cost: Up to 95% input cost with native caching claimed for large, stable documents with warm cache hits. This is not a measured total session bill; output tokens and service fees are excluded or not specified.
  • Latency: <90 ms pipeline p95; not end-to-end.
  • Accuracy or quality: Not found in cited source.

The token claims cover compression calls and large-document samples. They do not measure all messages, output tokens, or total agent-session savings. The latency figure covers the compression pipeline only. These claims use different workloads; compression fees and cache policy matter.

Sources: Product and benchmark summary; vendor cost and scope details; compression API.
