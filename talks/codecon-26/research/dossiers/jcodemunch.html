Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

jCodeMunch

  • Type: Symbol-level code retrieval.
  • Reduction purpose: Main, alongside precise code retrieval. The product retrieves symbols to reduce code-reading tokens.
  • Savings scope: The −96.2% claim covers tokens per lookup in 15 retrieval tests; it does not cover complete sessions. A separate 50-iteration agent workflow test reports −5.7% mean cost, −7.2% output tokens, −10.5% cache creation tokens, −6.0% mean duration, and +8 pp successful iterations. Each variant has 25 runs; these results are specific to that task.
  • Baseline and test: 15 lookups in 3 repositories versus grep and read.
  • Token volume: 96.2% fewer tokens per lookup.
  • Cost: Not found in cited source.
  • Latency: Not found in cited source.
  • Accuracy or quality: Not found in cited source.

A lookup is not a complete agent task.

Sources: Product documentation and lookup benchmark; agent workflow test.
