Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

Spotify Shunt

  • Type: Claude Code plugin that delegates large reads and code generation to a worker through Spotify Portal.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. Shunt delegates I/O-heavy work to reduce tokens in Claude's context.
  • Savings scope: Three selected bulk-read comparisons in the parent context. The figures exclude worker tokens and do not measure complete agent sessions or workflows. Targeted reads and small files can pass through without delegation.
  • Baseline: Spotify compared direct reads with Shunt on three bulk-read scenarios in a 162,000-line Java repository.
  • Token volume: Spotify reports 82–94% fewer tokens in Claude's context, with a 90% mean. The three comparisons are 33,684 → 5,737, 75,990 → 4,148, and 16,221 → 821.
  • Cost: n/a. The comparison does not establish total cost including the worker.
  • Latency: n/a.
  • Accuracy: n/a. No comparable task-success figure is reported in this benchmark.

Worker tokens are separate from tokens saved in the parent context. The code-generation example does not give a comparable token delta. A separate Shunt Direct adaptation is not the official Spotify plugin; the upstream benchmark does not establish its performance.

Sources: Spotify plugin and benchmark; Spotify engineering article.
