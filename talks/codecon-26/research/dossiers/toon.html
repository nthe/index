Product research note · survey snapshot 14 Nov 2026

TOON

  • Type: Structured data format.
  • Reduction purpose: Main. TOON encodes JSON data for token-efficient LLM input.
  • Savings scope: The −42.6% compares average input tokens for 244 retrieval questions on four models against formatted JSON. It is not a complete agent session or workflow. The separate paper tests agentic benchmarks; it reports up to −18% tokens in those tests.
  • Baseline and test: Project retrieval benchmark versus JSON representation.
  • Token volume: 42.6% fewer tokens.
  • Cost: Not measured.
  • Latency: Not measured.
  • Accuracy or quality: 72.2% versus 71.4% retrieval accuracy.

Format and task matter. Separate research reports up to 18% fewer tokens and about 9 percentage points lower accuracy on agent tasks: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29676.
